No account, no tracking

Stoker has no accounts. The site sets one cookie, which holds your light-or-dark choice, and keeps your acknowledgement of the disclaimer in your browser's own storage. There are no analytics scripts and no advertising trackers.

What the servers see

Like any website, the site and the API record each request (its address, browser and the page or endpoint asked for) in Cloudflare's logs, kept briefly to run the service and to stop abuse. The API may read the country a request comes from, as Cloudflare reports it, to decide which features are offered where.

What the chain keeps

Launches, trades, wallet addresses and payments are read from the Solana blockchain, which is public and permanent; the site shows them but cannot change or delete them. A coin's image and metadata are stored on Arweave at launch, where they are public and permanent too.

Who else is involved

The site runs on Cloudflare; chain data comes from Helius and public Solana RPC nodes; prices and routes come from Jupiter; permanent storage goes through ArDrive Turbo to Arweave; your wallet is provided by its own maker. Each of these sees what it needs to serve the request and has its own privacy terms.
