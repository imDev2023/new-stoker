What Stoker is

Stoker (stoker.markets) is a website and an API that build Solana transactions for Raydium's LaunchLab and CPMM programs and show what the Solana blockchain records. Stoker holds no funds and none of your keys. Every transaction is signed in your own wallet, which shows you what it does before you sign.

Copy Protection, risk labels and the figures on a coin's page are drawn from the chain on a best-effort basis and can be wrong or late. Read the risk disclosure before you launch or trade.

Fees and payments

Fees are set on-chain and listed, with their figures, on the Fees page. Half of what the platform collects on a coin's trades is forwarded to its creator in the coin's Quote Asset, as the public Ledger records; there is nothing to claim and no promise of a minimum. The platform may change its fee with notice under Announcements.
