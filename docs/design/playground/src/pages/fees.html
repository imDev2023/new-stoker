Fees

Every fee, read from the chain. Half of what the platform collects on a coin's trades is its Creator's.

Every fee What the platform does with its share

  1. Creating a coin

    No platform fee

    Solana charges the launch transaction's network fee and the rent of the coin's accounts; the review on the launch form shows the figure. A First Buy pays a curve trade's fees like any trade, and a Reward Launch costs nothing more to create.

    Who pays
    The Creator
    When
    At launch
    In
    SOL
    Who receives it
    Solana

  2. Graduation Fee

    Taken out of the Creator's earnings on the coin, never on top of them, from the next Creator Forward on until paid, and never more than the Creator is owed at the time. Each part is a graduation_fee entry on the Ledger.

    Who pays
    The Creator, from their earnings
    When
    Once the coin graduates
    In
    The coin's Quote Asset
    Who receives it
    The platform

  3. Platform Fee on the curve

    Half of it is forwarded to the coin's Creator as Creator Earnings, sent to their wallet with nothing to claim. The platform keeps the other half.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    Every curve trade
    In
    The coin's Quote Asset
    Who receives it
    The platform and the Creator

  4. Creator Fee on the curve

    LaunchLab's on-chain creator share. None on Stoker: Creators are paid through Creator Earnings instead.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    Every curve trade
    In
    The coin's Quote Asset
    Who receives it
    The Creator's LaunchLab vault

  5. Raydium's curve fee

    The LaunchLab program's own fee, set by Raydium on the Quote Asset's config.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    Every curve trade
    In
    The coin's Quote Asset
    Who receives it
    Raydium

  6. Referral share

    Only where the Quote Asset's Raydium config allows a share fee. SOL's does; most others, every tokenized stock among them, allow none. Never on a pool trade.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    A curve trade opened through a referral link
    In
    The coin's Quote Asset
    Who receives it
    The referrer

  7. Pool creator fee

    The platform is every graduated pool's creator and collects it. Raydium keeps a share at collection; half of what arrives is forwarded to the coin's Creator.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    Every pool trade
    In
    The asset going into the pool
    Who receives it
    The platform and the Creator

  8. Pool trade fee

    Raydium's CPMM fee. Part of it belongs to the pool's liquidity providers; every graduated pool's liquidity is locked for good with the platform holding its Fee Key, so that part reaches the platform.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    Every pool trade
    In
    The asset going into the pool
    Who receives it
    The platform and Raydium

  9. Reward Tax

    1%, 3% or 5% of every transfer

    Only on a Reward Launch, at the rate its Creator fixed at launch, which nobody can change. Paid to the coin's holders in Reward Rounds.

    Who pays
    Whoever sends the coin
    When
    Every transfer
    In
    The coin
    Who receives it
    The coin's holders

  10. Buying with SOL a coin paired with another asset

    The swap's own fee, if any

    The buy swaps SOL into the Quote Asset through Jupiter first, spending only what the swap is sure to deliver: up to 0.3% under the current price. Anything beyond stays in your wallet.

    Who pays
    The trader
    When
    A SOL buy of a coin not paired with SOL
    In
    SOL
    Who receives it
    Jupiter and its pools

  11. Swapping on the Swap and Stocks pages

    0.5% of every swap

    Jupiter's referral fee. Jupiter keeps a fifth of it; the platform receives the rest. Nothing is forwarded to a Creator: no coin is involved.

    Who pays
    The swapper
    When
    Every swap on the Swap or Stocks pages
    In
    The asset swapped
    Who receives it
    The platform and Jupiter

  12. Network fees and rent

    Solana's own

    Every transaction pays Solana's fee, and one that opens an account pays its rent. Shown on every review before you sign.

    Who pays
    Whoever signs
    When
    Every transaction
    In
    SOL
    Who receives it
    Solana
