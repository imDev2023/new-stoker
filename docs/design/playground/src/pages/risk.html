Coins launched here are speculative and can lose all their value. Most new coins do. Nobody sets or supports a coin's price: it comes from what other people pay for it, and it can fall to nothing at any time.

How a coin trades

A coin starts on a bonding curve, where every buy raises the price and every sell lowers it. Once the curve has sold out, Raydium moves the coin into a liquidity pool, where it trades at whatever price buyers and sellers agree. A coin's pool liquidity is locked at Graduation; its price is not.

The asset a coin is priced in

Each coin is priced in an asset its creator chose: SOL, a stablecoin, a tokenized stock or another asset Raydium quotes. Some of those assets are issued by companies that can freeze or move them, some can be changed by their issuer, and some trade thinly, so a coin's value moves with its asset as well as with itself. What is known about each asset is on its page under Assets, as risk labels read from the chain.

Creators, early buyers and taxes

A coin's creator may hold a large share of it and may sell at any time; so may the wallets that bought in its first moments. A coin's page shows the creator's holding, the early buyers and the largest wallets as the chain records them. A Reward Launch carries a transfer tax of 1%, 3% or 5% on every transfer of the coin, taken in the coin itself.

Fees

Every trade pays a fee to Raydium and to the platform, on the curve and after Graduation, and a coin's creator pays a Graduation Fee out of their earnings once it graduates. Every fee, who pays it and who receives it, with its figure read from the chain, is on the Fees page, the one place the site shows a fee figure. Half of what the platform collects on a coin's trades is forwarded to its creator, and every payment is on the public Ledger. Fees are set on-chain and can only change with notice under Announcements.

Technology

Stoker builds transactions for Raydium's programs on Solana and shows what the chain records; it never holds your funds or your keys. A signed transaction cannot be reversed. The programs, the Solana network, your wallet and the data providers the site reads from are outside Stoker's control and can fail, pause or change.

Who may use the site

The site is not offered to people in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada or sanctioned countries, nor to anyone where launching or trading these coins is not allowed. Nothing on this site is financial, legal or tax advice. Questions go to info@stoker.markets.
